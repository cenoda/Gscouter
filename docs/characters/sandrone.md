# Sandrone

> Character on the gcsim fork (`docs/fork.md`) · Last updated: 2026-10-28

Sandrone (5★ Cryo, 6.7). Upstream gcsim: no PR. Scored in the experimental tier (design §5.4); the fork's rules and regression runs are in `docs/fork.md`.

## 1. On the fork

**Source.** Ours (2026-10-27): multipliers from the pipeline; mechanics from the game's ability and gadget data (`ConfigAbility_Avatar_MarionetteNew`, `ConfigAvatar_MarionetteNew`, `ConfigGadget_SkillObj_MarionetteNew`; MarionetteNew is her internal name). Fagio is a task loop driven by its Decoding Power, not a gadget: in game its sweeping fire, condensed beams and Power Overdrive shots are bullets Sandrone fires while her charged attack is held. Decoding Power gains 3.2 every 0.2 s and 17 per beam count (both halved at C1) and is set to 100 after the 3rd count (C1: 6th), so a charged attack from 0 fires 3 condensed beams at C0 and 6 at C1. In Radiance: Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl the beams, the second Prism Shot and the burst's ray are replaced by direct Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl hits with their own multipliers, which apply no element; the engine's Polestar Field and Radiance pattern (Odette's) is reused unchanged

**Frame data.** Every frame is estimated: no community count exists (KQM quick guide, KQM library). Charged attack entry 21 and end 18 frames and skill → charged attack 22 are set so that a C E pair costs about 1 s besides Decoding mode, and 3[C E] at C0 and 2[C E] at C1 fill the 10–13 s of field time the KQM quick guide reports; normal attacks follow Dehya's N1–N3; burst animation 100 frames. From the data, not estimated: Fagio's timings (first beam at 0.85 s then every 1 s, sweeping fire every 0.3 s, Power Overdrive shots every 0.4 s, Decoding Power rates and decay, the skill's drain of 70 then 10 per 0.1 s), Prism Shot and burst hit times, ICD and gauges. Calibration against [HoYoLAB 46338256](https://www.hoyolab.com/article/46338256) (C0 Sandrone with The Unforged R1, C1 Yae Miko, C0 Escoffier, C0 Odette; Sandrone's and Odette's artifacts as posted, Yae's and Escoffier's weapons inferred from their base ATK and their stats matched to the posted panels; level 100 dummy, 10% RES, 120 s; every member's panel stats reproduced exactly): team 184.2k against 209.1k (−11.9%); Sandrone 11.07M against 13.28M (−16.7%), Odette −3.6%, Yae −16%, Escoffier +27%; strongest hit, the ray with 10 Refined Tactics stacks, 622k against 658k (−5.4%) with the burst right after the first skill, 521k when it ends her field time. The post gives no rotation (ours is KQM's 3[C E] with the burst after the first skill). Polestar Field stacks are 8–9 in most of her hits; with the multiplier held at 12 stacks (a test build) the team gives 200.2k (−4.2%), but Odette then overshoots (+8.8%) while Sandrone and Yae stay about 9% low, which points to field time per member, i.e. the unknown rotation. Gap investigation (2026-10-28, fork `76e8116`, same config: 184.3k), no cause found in our code: (1) Polestar Field stacks follow the game's rule as KQM's Stellar Glimmer guide states it and the data shows it (`TeamAbility_StarSuperconductor`: a hit counter capped at 12, copied to the party and reset every 4 s): only Cryo and Electro hits that apply an element count, as limited by ICD, one per attack, and direct Stellar-Conduct hits (which apply none) do not; the sim records about 230 such applications per 120 s (Escoffier's Frosty Parfait 52, Sandrone's sweeping fire 45 and Prism Shot 1 16, Yae's Sesshou Sakura 40, Odette's dance moves 51, the rest bursts), about 8 per 4 s window, so 8–9 stacks is right for this rotation. The post's strongest hit, the ray at 657,721, equals ours at 11 stacks (656k, −0.3%; ours fire at 8–9 stacks: 605k and 622k), so the per-hit formula, multipliers (checked against the game's talent tables) and buffs agree, and the game reached 11 stacks at least once. (2) Sandrone's per-hit buffs are at their caps in the sim: CRIT Rate over 100% (Disenchantment 16% and Cryo resonance 15%), Odette's Marvelous Splendor 4 stacks (+60%), Heart of the Furnace +50%, Yae's C1 +50%, Disenchantment +40%, Stellar Jubilee 14% from both Odette and Sandrone. (3) Field time does not explain it: the post's reaction tracker shows Yae triggering Stellar-Conduct 38 times (ours 42) and Odette once (ours 1), and Sandrone's field time (76 s, about 12.6 s per rotation) is already at the top of the KQM guide's 10–13 s. Rotations move all members together: KQM's order (Yae 3[E], Odette 2[E] (Q), Escoffier (Q) E, Sandrone) 181.0k (Sandrone −17.7%, Yae −19.0%, Odette −5.1%, Escoffier +25.2%); 4[C E] 176.1k (−14.0%, −25.1%, −19.8%, +17.2%); a second burst slot 186.8k (−16.4%, −13.1%, −2.9%, +29.1%). (4) Escoffier's +27% is per hit, not field time: each skill runs 21 Frosty Parfait ticks over about 22 s, so any rotation up to 22 s covers the whole 120 s (101 ticks). Her base ATK (1,087) fits only Calamity Queller among polearms (and Yae's 1,014 only Skyward Atlas among catalysts); the posted panels show 0% Cryo and Electro DMG Bonus although both weapons give 12%, so the panels apparently leave out weapon passives. The remaining gap (Sandrone and Yae about 16% low with every per-hit factor checked) is not attributable with the post's data; it needs a per-source breakdown or a video of the run. Weapon ratio against Prydwen's table (as quoted by genshin-build.com): A Teaspoon of Transcendence R1 over Blackcliff Slasher R5 is 1.18 for the team and 1.39 for Sandrone (Prydwen 1.18 and 1.39), over Tidal Shadow R5 1.16 and 1.33 (Prydwen 1.12 and 1.26). Constellations: Sandrone's damage is 1.43× at C2 and 2.61× at C6 of C0, against about +40% and "100%+" in [HoYoLAB 45845639](https://www.hoyolab.com/article/45845639)

**Known problems.** No plunge; the hovermech's movement and sprint are not modeled (the hover lasts until the next action, at most 6 s); Prism Shots always find a target (the game needs one within 20 m); the C6 cluster beam stops when Decoding mode ends, which the data leaves open; Stellar Jubilee's base DMG bonus adds to Odette's and the Cryo Traveler's in a shared team, the engine's existing pattern. Forged by the Golden Melody, Blade of Atonement, A Thousand Blazing Suns and Verdict rank far below Prydwen's table on the calibration team; the first two need Sandrone to trigger Stellar-Conduct herself, which a Cryo-heavy team seldom lets her do (the showcase's reaction tracker shows none for her), so the gap is the team, not checked further

No public number exists for the other three teams (searched 2026-10-28: the KQM guide gives none and no gcsim links; GenshinBuilds, GameWith, topuplist, AllThingsHow and fandomwire team pages give ratings only).
- Known problem, left for later (2026-10-28): the fork's calibration gap, −11.9% for the team against HoYoLAB 46338256 (Sandrone −16.7%, Yae Miko −16%, Escoffier +27%), cause unknown; the leading hypothesis is the Polestar Field stack count (8–9 in the sim, while the post's strongest ray implies 11). These teams carry it: scores may rise when it is found. Not investigated in this batch, and the engine is unchanged.

- These teams are short of energy in the sim. No member with a burst in the rotation can burst every rotation at an ER the rules allow, Sandrone included: at 166% (the top of her table's +1 Cryo row, 130–165%) she still skips or waits, and her score falls as ER rises. So every burst is cast when ready, which comes to every other rotation, and Sandrone follows the guide's advice for an ER need above 140%: no ER beyond the standard's (111%, the two freed rolls going to ATK% under the standard's caps), burst when available. That scores highest of the variants tried (in the Sucrose team 64.7k looped, against 55.5k at 166% with her burst written into every rotation). The looped score keeps only 76–80% of the one pass, which starts from full energy.
- The others: C0 Alyosha (the limited-roster team names no constellation; no harvested team has him) follows his KQM quick guide with KQM-standard substats: Favonius Lance R3, 4pc Heart of the Furnace, ER/ATK%/CRIT Rate, 220.1% ER, inside the guide's 195–225% for C0 with Favonius and no other Electro member, yet even at 264% he waits about 4 s per minute for energy. C6 Beidou and C6 Diona are batch 2's builds; Charlotte (C6, Thrilling Tales R5, 4pc Ocean-Hued Clam, 111% ER, skips bursts even at 166%) and Xilonen (C0, Favonius Sword, 4pc Scroll) are copied from harvested teams named in `build.txt`; Sucrose is batch 1's.
- Energy shortfall investigated (2026-10-28; energy per member and source over the looped 150–450 s, 10 seeds; no change made):
  - Enemy particles are not the cause: the KQM standards set gcsim's enemy energy to `energy every interval=480,720 amount=1`, the common conditions' line (design §3.2), and the guides' ER tables use the same standard.
  - Sandrone's own particles (one per 2.5 s from condensed beams and Prism Shots) agree with her guide where its assumptions hold: in batches 1 and 2 her ER for a burst every rotation (116–155%) falls inside the table.
  - The table assumes Favonius holders (without them it adds 10–20%), and these teams' Charlotte and Sucrose carry Thrilling Tales from their harvested builds. With Favonius Codex R3 on both, Sandrone bursts every rotation at 166% (0.06 s per minute of waits; at 149.6% she still waits 0.7 s), the top of her +1 Cryo row; in the Beidou / Diona / Sucrose team, 166% leaves 0.11 s. But that ER costs CRIT: the Charlotte team then scores 75.4k looped against 95.3k at 111% with the burst when ready, so the guide's advice (above 140%, build no ER and burst when available) is also the best score here.
  - About a third of Sandrone's energy in the Charlotte team overflows while her burst waits for its one check after the first skill. Checking after each C E pair instead (her guide allows the burst between charged attacks) does not help: 91.2k, 99.5k, 65.4k and 65.4k looped against 95.3k, 104.4k, 64.9k and 69.2k, since a late burst wastes the teammates' buffs.
  - C0 Alyosha's gap (264% still waits, the guide gives 195–225%) is gcsim's particle timing: his skill's 5 particles arrive 118 frames after it, and E then Q swaps him out at frame 82, so all of them count off field (×0.6; 299 of his 580 energy per 300 s). The guide calls EQ self-funneling, which suggests it counts them on field. This is how gcsim treats every character's particles, upstream included, so it is left as is.
- No public number exists for any of these teams (searched 2026-10-28: Icy Veins, GenshinBuilds, playnews and other team pages give ratings only). The fork's calibration gap applies here too. Only team directories and this document changed, so no regression run was needed.

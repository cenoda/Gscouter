# gcsim fork

> Status: in use for the experimental tier · Last updated: 2026-10-29

The experimental tier (design §5.4) scores characters and weapons upstream gcsim lacks on a Gscouter fork of gcsim. This document covers how the fork is kept, how verified scores are kept from moving, and what is on it. Each character's implementation notes and library teams are in `docs/characters/<name>.md`.

## 1. How the fork is kept

- A fork of gcsim under the project's GitHub account, used through a `replace` directive in `go.mod`: the `gscouter` branch of [cenoda/gcsim](https://github.com/cenoda/gcsim/tree/gscouter). It is upstream `main` plus one group of commits per added character, so a gcsim update is a rebase of those groups.
  - A group taken from an upstream PR starts with the PR squashed into one commit under its author's name, unchanged; our fixes follow as separate commits, so they can be offered back to the PR.
  - After a rebase: `go mod edit -replace` to the new commit, `internal/enka/gen.sh` (Enka mapping and names), `library.ForkOnly` and `library.ForkOnlyWeapons` updated for characters and weapons added to the fork or merged upstream, `share.GcsimFork` set to the new fork commit (a test checks it against `go.mod`), the fork's own `go test ./...` (its frame and ability tests cover every character), and the regression run below.
- Each added character comes, in order of preference, from an open upstream PR (Nefer, Linnea, Kachina have one) or from our own implementation. Multipliers come from gcsim's data pipeline either way.
- Each added character records its source (PR or ours), where each action's frame data comes from (measured, taken from the PR, or estimated from a similar character) and its known gaps. The result page links them.
- Our own implementations are also sent upstream as PRs. Review there improves accuracy, and every merge ends the fork's cost for that character.
- When upstream merges a character, its fork commit is dropped and its teams move to the verified tier after revalidation. Scores may shift; results already record the gcsim version.

## 2. Verified scores must not move

The server and the browser build (design §4.7) run both tiers on the same gcsim, so the fork must leave every verified team's score unchanged (the regression runs in §2 below; design §5.3, step 4 of the table). Engine changes a new character needs, such as Vodyanitsa's Wandering Vortex, are switched on only when that character is in the team, the way gcsim already enables Stellar Swirl through `Flags.Custom`.

- Because of that, a team that differs is not yet a regression: rerun it on both builds, at least 20 times each. Only a difference that repeats counts and must be fixed; a value that appears once on one side and never again is recorded as that nondeterminism. `cmd/compare/build.sh` builds `cmd/validate` on the fork, on upstream (go.mod without the replace directive) and on other fork commits, without changing go.mod; `validate -json` writes every report with the gcsim it was built with, and `cmd/compare` compares two runs field by field. Given several runs per side, it tells a regression (a result that repeats on one side and never appears on the other) from that nondeterminism (results seen once); with one run per side it lists the teams that differ, to rerun with `-cache ""` (the cache would repeat the first result). On an upstream build, `cmd/validate` skips teams with fork-only characters.

### Shared code

Nefer's PR changes how Verdant Dew is collected so that her P2 and C4 can speed it up. The partial dew counter becomes a float and each frame adds 1 plus the sum of registered rate mods; only Nefer registers one. Without Nefer the sum is 0 and the counter reaches 146 on the same frame as before; Lauma and Columbina teams were part of the regression run. Linnea and Kachina change nothing outside their packages; all their event subscriptions are made when they are in the party. ## 5. Weapons on the fork

### Hymn of the Maelstrom

Hymn of the Maelstrom (`76e8116`, ours, 2026-10-28), Vodyanitsa's signature catalyst (weapon 14524, internal name Bludnye). Base stats, names and generated files from the pipeline; the passive from the game's `Weapon_Catalyst_Bludnye` (`ConfigAbility_Weapon_Catalyst2`), its params from EquipAffix 114524 mapped by `ConfigAffix_Catalyst`. Healing Bonus +4% (R5 8%, wearer); each heal the wearer performs (also off-field; healing received does not count) adds a stack, 10 s each, max 3, a heal at the cap refreshing all. Per stack the wearer gets +4% Max HP (R5 8%) and the active member +0.4% ATK per 1,000 of the wearer's Max HP over 40,000 (R5 0.8%), capped at 8% per stack (R5 16%, reached at 60,000), so three stacks give up to 24% (R5 48%). A Freeze or Stellar Swirl on an opponent by any party member multiplies both by 1.75 for 5 s. In a debug sample with Vodyanitsa at R1 and R5, every part fired and expired as the data says; guides agree on the numbers except a pre-release page (8% Healing Bonus, a 5% ATK cap, 8 s). No engine change; the new weapon key moved the 148 weapon keys after it (Ibis Piercer to Xiphos' Moonlight) up by one, which the regression run covers.
- Known problems: whether the game counts direct Stellar Swirl hits (Odette's, Sandrone's, Vesna's) as Stellar Swirl reactions for the 1.75 state is not checked (gcsim emits no reaction for them, so they do not trigger it); guides word the 8% ATK limit ambiguously, and the data's cap per stack is what makes the AllThingsHow comparison 25% high (`docs/characters/vodyanitsa.md` §1).

### Heretic's Molten Blade, New Bough, Winter's Heavy Heart

Heretic's Molten Blade (11435), New Bough (11437) and Winter's Heavy Heart (14437) (`b049a48`, Heretic's Molten Blade's movement `b029c45`, ours, 2026-10-28), three 4-star weapons a showcase could wear but gcsim lacked ("weapon 11435 is not supported by gcsim yet"). Base stats, names and generated files from the pipeline; each passive from the game's ability data with the EquipAffix params for R1–R5, mapped to the ability's specials by `ConfigAffix_Sword` and `ConfigAffix_Catalyst`. No engine change.
- Heretic's Molten Blade (`Weapon_Sword_SerpentTooth`, `ConfigAbility_Weapon_Sword2`): the wearer's Elemental Skill, outside a 14 s cooldown, gives ATK +18% (R5 36%) at once for 14 s; from 1 s on the bonus is `min + (max − min) × distance / 7` up to 36% (R5 72%) at 7 m per second. Leaving the field removes it; the cooldown stays. The distance is the movement the rotation reports (`movement()` or an action's `movement` param), summed while the buff is up and read every second, as Iansan's burst counts it: the library's Iansan teams report 9 m/s, which gives the maximum; a rotation that reports none keeps the minimum.
- New Bough (`Weapon_Sword_SpikedStake`): the Elemental Skill opens a 12 s window; a hit by the wearer on an opponent in it adds a stack (1 s ICD, max 3, one 6 s duration refreshed by each gain, also off-field). Per stack ATK +4% and EM +20 (R5 8%, 40); in Radiance (`_IS_IN_STARMODE`: the wearer's Polestar Field or Radiance Swirl status, as Sandrone, Qiqi and Vesna read it) instead ATK +6% and Stellar DMG +8% (R5 12%, 16%), switching as the state changes. Every hit credited to the wearer counts, gadgets and summons included; whether the game's `onHittingOther` counts those is not checked.
- Winter's Heavy Heart (`Weapon_Catalyst_FrostScepter`, `ConfigAbility_Weapon_Catalyst2`): counts the party's Cryo members, wearer included (at most 4), then Electro members up to 4 in total. EM +24 per Cryo and ATK +4.8% per Electro (R5 48, 9.6%); in Radiance instead EM +20 and Stellar DMG +6% per counted member (R5 40, 12%). The game recounts every 2 s; the party never changes in gcsim, so it is counted once.
- Checked with debug samples at R1 and R5 for each: the values, the cooldown and swap removal, stack gains, ICD and expiry, and the Radiance switch.

### Fork-only weapons

Fork-only weapons are listed in `library.ForkOnlyWeapons` (gcsim keys; now `hereticsmoltenblade`, `hymnofthemaelstrom`, `newbough` and `wintersheavyheart`), next to `library.ForkOnly`, and count like a fork-only character. A library team whose `build.txt` equips one (any gcsim name or shortcut) needs `tier: experimental` and `gcsim_fork`; `cmd/import` and `cmd/harvest` set both; `cmd/validate` skips it on an upstream build. A result where the player's showcase build uses one gets the experimental badge and a note that a weapon is not in gcsim yet (`warn.experimental_weapon`, every site language), whatever the team's tier; without it the result is unchanged. A showcase character with Hymn failed before ("weapon 14524 is not supported by gcsim yet"). The server embeds only `team.yaml` and `rotation.gcsl`, so the rule on library builds is checked where they are read from disk: `cmd/validate`, the importers and a library test that loads every team with its `build.txt` (no library team uses Hymn now). For the same reason a library team's own page shows the badge from its tier, but not the weapon note.

## 7. Witch's Revelation for Wriothesley and Cyno

_Checked 2026-10-29 against upstream `main` and the two PRs' diffs. On the fork since `ee3fc92`._

Version 6.7 gave seven characters a Witch's Revelation upgrade, unlocked by a world quest once the character is level 70 or higher: Wriothesley, Yae Miko, Cyno, Qiqi, Diona, Beidou and Yumemizuki Mizuki ([AllThingsHow](https://allthings.how/genshin-impact-6-7-character-buffs-and-witchs-revelation-luna-viii/)). Wriothesley's and Cyno's upgrades let them deal Stellar-Conduct DMG. Like Qiqi's, they need a Polestar Field: while inside one they are in the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state. Neither of them enables the reaction, so a team still needs an enabler (Odette, Sandrone or the Cryo Traveler) and Cryo and Electro hits to make the field. gcsim models being inside the field as having `reactable.PolestarFieldKey` active, as Qiqi's `stellar.go` does.

- **Wriothesley**: what changes and our C2 fix are in `docs/characters/wriothesley.md`. The PR adds two attack tags (`AttackTagWrioSSCN5`, `AttackTagWrioSSCCA`) after `AttackTagVarkaSpecial`, which moves every later tag up by one, the reaction tags among them; the regression run (§2) checks that this changes nothing.
- **Cyno**: in the field, Featherfall Judgment's Duststalker Bolt becomes direct Electro Stellar-Conduct DMG: 200% plus 600% of EM, against 100% plus 250% of EM outside it. The PR's comment on `a4BoltSSC` still says 250%, so check the 600% against the game data before relying on it.
  - Outside the field the upgrade still changes his kit: casting the skill outside Pactsworn Pathclearer puts him in it for 6 s. That state cannot be extended, and it queues A1 at 234 frames (a `TODO` in the PR).
  - In the field, C1 to C6 change as well. For example, C1's Together We Rise gives +200 EM and passes to the next active character, and C2 gives +16% Stellar-Conduct DMG per stack, up to 5 stacks.
  - The PR also fixes the N4 → charged attack cancel inside the burst (from N4's first hitmark to its second), which applies to every Cyno team.
  - No KQM guide covers his Stellar-Conduct play yet; [Gaming ProMax](https://gamingpromax.com/cyno-stellar-conduct-build/) uses his enhanced skill hits with Odette, Sandrone or the Cryo Traveler.

**The upgrade is on.** Both PRs read a `revelation` character parameter that defaults to 1, as Qiqi's does. A profile cannot show whether the quest is done, so the library follows gcsim's default, as the Vesna teams do for Qiqi (`docs/characters/vesna.md`). A team whose rotation breaks with it sets `revelation=0` in its params and says why. Existing teams took the new scores (§2, result for `ee3fc92`).

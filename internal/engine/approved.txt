# (docs/engine.md §2). Only the owner approves: a team's score moves
# (within 1% of gcsim) when it moves to the engine.

# (YYYY-MM-DD), then, for a team approved from group B,

# group A holds while the team stays verified in group A of the pinned
# release (release/manifest.json); one from group B, for its release only.
# cmd/pinengine takes off the approvals a release drops, and adds group A
# with -approve-a after the owner's yes.
